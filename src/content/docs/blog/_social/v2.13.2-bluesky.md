Beluga AI v2.13.2: `beluga dev` runs your agent against a mock LLM with real observability, so you iterate on prompts and routing without provider credits. `beluga eval run` measures agent quality against a suite from the CLI.

2/3
With v2.13.2 the full DX loop is in the CLI: `beluga init` (scaffold) → `beluga new` (add components) → `beluga dev` (iterate) → `beluga eval` (measure). No breaking changes. go get github.com/lookatitude/beluga-ai/v2@v2.13.2
