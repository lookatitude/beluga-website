Beluga AI v2.13.2: a real dev loop for agents. `beluga run` executes your program, `beluga dev` runs it with hot reload, `beluga test` wires in a mock LLM so tests don't hit real APIs. Observability is bootstrapped automatically.

`beluga eval` (v2.13.0) scaffolds eval definitions and runs them against your agent — pairs with the `eval/judge` package. v2.11.1, v2.13.1, v2.13.2 are CI/dep patches. go get github.com/lookatitude/beluga-ai/v2@v2.13.2
