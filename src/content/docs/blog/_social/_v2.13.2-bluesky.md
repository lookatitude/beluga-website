Beluga AI v2.13.0 shipped `beluga eval`: run your agent against a dataset, compute metrics, emit a report. Built-in `exact_match` and `latency` metrics, with LLM judges on the roadmap.

2/3
Exec-once IPC: the evaluator spawns your agent binary once per sample row, passes input through `BELUGA_EVAL_SAMPLE_JSON`, and reads the result back. OTel-instrumented, and `--format junit` plugs into `dorny/test-reporter` for PR annotations. go get github.com/lookatitude/beluga-ai/v2@v2.13.2
