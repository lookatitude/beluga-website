1/6
Beluga AI v2.13.2 is out. The headline shipped in v2.13.0: the Beluga Eval CLI. Scaffold and run evaluation suites for your agents and pipelines without writing your own harness.

2/6
`beluga eval init <name>` lays down a suite skeleton — cases, judges, config — in your repo. `beluga eval run` executes the suite and reports pass/fail per case. Three judges ship by default: `Contains`, `ExactMatch`, and `Similarity`.

3/6
Custom judges implement the `judge.Judge` interface. Anything you can express in Go — a regex check, a structured-output validator, a model-graded judge calling another provider — plugs in the same way the built-ins do.

4/6
`beluga eval run` returns CI-compatible exit codes. Wire it into a GitHub Actions step or any CI runner and a regression on your eval suite fails the build. Evaluation stops being a manual notebook exercise and becomes part of the same gate as your tests.

5/6
v2.13.1 and v2.13.2 are documentation and audit-docs updates — no API changes, no breaking changes. Safe to upgrade from any v2.13.x.

6/6
Update with: go get github.com/lookatitude/beluga-ai/v2@v2.13.2
